FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Door-to-Door transportation?

Door-to-Door transportation means the driver provides assistance from the pickup door to the vehicle, helps with safe boarding, and assists from the vehicle to the destination entrance. It is helpful for wheelchair users, seniors, passengers with limited mobility, and anyone who needs more support than curbside pickup.

Who is Door-to-Door service for?

It is designed for passengers going to medical appointments, rehab, dialysis, adult day programs, family visits, airports, or private trips when they need help getting safely between the building entrance and the vehicle.

Are the vehicles properly equipped?

Yes. Our wheelchair-accessible vans are properly equipped for ramp access and use Q’Straint wheelchair securement systems for safer transportation.

How do I book a ride?

Call 617-251-7049 or email mexpress.mass@gmail.com with pickup address, destination, appointment time, return trip details, and whether the passenger uses a wheelchair.
